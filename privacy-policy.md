# Privacy Policy — Collective 1:3, Y/OUR Archive

Effective: October 16, 2026

Collective 1:3 (Y/OUR Archive) is run by Abel Chamberlin ("I", "me"). This explains, in plain language, what the app stores, who can see it, and how to delete it.

## What the app stores
- **Account:** your email address, a password (stored securely by the login service; I can't see it), and your first and last name.
- **Your references:** the photos you upload and the details you add (title, category, material, designer, project, location, Private/Public).
- **Your activity in the archive:** bookmarks and folders (and whether a folder is public), saved people, saved collections, people you block, and reports you send.
- **On your phone only:** a few settings (for example, that you've seen the opening page) and older "on this device" photos that were never uploaded.

The location of a reference is only what you type in. The app doesn't use your phone's GPS.

## What I don't do
- No ads, no tracking, no analytics profiles.
- I don't sell or rent your information to anyone.
- No follower counts or public activity: nobody can see who bookmarked, saved, blocked or reported them.

## Who can see what
- **Private references** (the default): only you.
- **Public references and public collections:** anyone using the app, credited with your **first and last name**. Your email is never shown.
- **Your name** becomes visible to others only once you have at least one public reference or public collection.
- **Bookmarks, saved people, saved collections, blocks and reports:** only you. Reports are also visible to the moderator (me), but never to the person reported.

## Where it's kept
Your data is stored with **Supabase** (database, file storage and login), which hosts it on secure servers on my behalf. Supabase only processes it to run the app. Photos are kept in private storage and are only shown through short-lived links to people allowed to see them.

## Seeing and correcting your information
You can see and change your references, their details and visibility, your collections and your saved items in the app at any time. To correct your name or email, or to ask what information is stored about you, email me (below).

## Do Not Track
The app doesn't track you across other apps or websites, so there is nothing for "Do Not Track" signals to switch off; it works the same either way.

## Keeping and deleting
Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. You can:
- make any reference or collection private, or delete it, at any time;
- delete your whole account in **Account → Delete account**, which permanently deletes your account, your references and photos, your collections and your saved items. It may take a short time to disappear from backups.

## Children
The app is not for children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact me and it will be deleted.

## Changes
If this policy changes in an important way, the app will tell you. The current version is always available in the app.

## Contact
Questions or requests about your data: **chamberlinarcharts@gmail.com**
