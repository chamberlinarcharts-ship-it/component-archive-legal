An archive of architectural components: a shared catalog of built details, documented by the people who notice them.

## Contact

## Common questions

**How do I delete my account?**
In the app, open **Account** (the character icon), scroll to the bottom and tap **Delete account**. Type DELETE to confirm. This permanently deletes your account, your uploads and their photos, your collections and your saved items.

**Who can see my photos?**
Uploads are **private** unless you choose **Public**. Public references and public collections can be seen by anyone using the app and are credited to you by name. Bookmarks, saved people, saved collections and blocks are always private.

**How do I report a photo or a person?**
On someone else's public photo, tap **report** at the bottom of the page. On someone's page, tap **block** (top right) and choose **Block and report**.

**How do I block someone?**
Open their page (tap "Documented by …" on one of their photos) and tap **block**. You can unblock people in **Account → Blocked people**.
